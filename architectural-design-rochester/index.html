Local knowledge

Planning in Rochester: what to know

Historic Rochester

The city centre, Star Hill to Sun Pier and New Road are conservation areas, and Historic Rochester has an Article 4 direction that removes the usual householder freedoms. Many buildings are also listed.

Backland and infill homes

Medway has a specific policy for new homes built behind or beside existing houses, covering access, overlooking and the size of plots and gardens. We test these early with a site layout.

Flats and HMOs

Converting houses to flats is judged against Medway's flat conversion policy, and an Article 4 direction requires permission for small HMOs in wards including Strood North & Frindsbury.

Local policies we design to

Applications are still decided under the Medway Local Plan (2003). Its replacement, the Medway Local Plan 2041, is being examined by a government inspector, and its draft policies are starting to carry weight.

  • BNE1 General Principles for Built DevelopmentThe main design test, used for extensions as Medway has no separate extensions policy.
  • BNE2 Amenity ProtectionProtects neighbours' light, outlook and privacy.
  • H6 Flat ConversionsSets the tests for converting houses into flats.
  • H7 Multiple OccupationControls HMOs, alongside an Article 4 direction in seven wards.
  • H9 Backland and Tandem DevelopmentApplies to new homes built behind or beside existing houses.
  • BNE12–BNE18 Conservation areas and listed buildingsProtect the character of Medway's historic areas and buildings.

FAQs

Common questions in Rochester

Do I need permission to replace windows in Rochester?

In Historic Rochester it may, because of the Article 4 direction, and it always does for a listed building. We'll check your property first.

Can you help with a listed building in Rochester?

Yes. We survey, draw and prepare the heritage statement, and apply for listed building consent and planning together where both are needed.

Can I build a house in my garden in Rochester?

Possibly, if access, plot size and neighbours' privacy work. Medway's backland policy sets the tests, and we'll check them before full drawings.
