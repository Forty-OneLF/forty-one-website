Local knowledge

Planning in Ramsgate: what to know

Conservation areas and Article 4

Much of the town is in the Ramsgate, Royal Esplanade or Montefiore conservation areas. On parts of streets such as Nelson Crescent, Paragon, Albion Hill and Prospect Terrace, an Article 4 direction removes the usual householder freedoms, so changes such as windows and doors can need permission. We check your address first.

Listed buildings

Ramsgate has a large number of listed buildings, and works inside as well as outside need listed building consent. We prepare the heritage statement and detail the work so it's acceptable to the conservation officer.

HMOs and new homes

An Article 4 direction across Thanet means a family house to small HMO change needs planning permission, and new homes pay a small coastal habitat contribution. Both are built into our applications.

Local policies we design to

Applications are decided under the Thanet Local Plan (adopted 2020) and, in Cliftonville, the Cliftonville Development Plan Document (2010). Work on a replacement plan has only just started.

  • QD02 General Design PrinciplesThe main design test, covering extensions and new homes, including building on garden land where it isn't harmful to the area.
  • QD03 Living ConditionsProtects neighbours' light, outlook and privacy, and sets the living standards for new homes.
  • HO19 Houses in Multiple OccupationNew HMOs are not permitted in the mapped parts of Cliftonville and Margate Central.
  • HO8 Cliftonville West and Margate CentralArea policy aimed at improving housing quality in these wards.
  • HE02 Development in Conservation AreasDevelopment must preserve or enhance the conservation area's character.
  • Kent Design GuideAdopted county-wide design guidance that the council also uses.

FAQs

Common questions in Ramsgate

Do I need permission to change windows in Ramsgate?

In many central and harbourside streets, yes. The conservation areas and an Article 4 direction remove the usual freedom to change windows and doors. We'll check your property and prepare the application if needed.

Can you help with a listed building in Ramsgate?

Yes. We survey the building, prepare drawings and a heritage statement, and apply for listed building consent alongside planning where both are needed.

How long does planning take with Thanet District Council?

The council aims to decide householder applications within eight weeks of validation. Listed building consent runs to a similar timescale, and we submit both together.
