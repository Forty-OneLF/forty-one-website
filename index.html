    From first call to building on site

    Every project starts with planning. If you need building regulations drawings as well, the technical stage follows straight on from the approved design, drawn by the same person.

    Planning

    Getting the design right and getting it approved.

    1. 01

      Initial call and quote

      Tell us what you want to do. We'll talk through what's likely to need permission and send a clear fixed-fee quote.

      You getA written quote and scope
    2. 02

      Site visit and measured survey

      We come to the property, measure it and photograph it, so every drawing is based on what is actually there.

      You getAccurate existing drawings
    3. 03

      The brief in detail

      We go through how you live or how the building needs to work, your budget and must-haves, and the planning constraints on the site.

      You getAn agreed design brief
    4. 04

      Design and drawings

      We develop the proposal with you, refining layouts and elevations until you're happy. CGIs can be added to help you see it.

      You getProposed plans and elevations

      Application submitted

      We prepare the forms, location plans and supporting statements and submit everything to the council on your behalf.

      Through to a decision

      We deal with the case officer, answer queries and agree amendments. Householder applications usually take around eight weeks.

    FAQs

    Common questions

    Do I need planning permission?

    Not always. Many extensions and loft conversions can go ahead under permitted development, though conservation areas, listed buildings and flats have extra rules. We'll tell you on the free call which route your project is likely to need, and a lawful development certificate can confirm it formally.

    How long does a planning application take?

    Councils aim to decide householder and smaller applications within eight weeks of validation, and larger schemes within thirteen. Allow a couple of weeks before that for the survey and drawings.

    How much will it cost?

    Every project is priced individually after the free call, as a written quote with the scope set out clearly, so you know where you stand before anything starts.

    Do I need building regulations drawings as well?

    Most building work needs building regulations approval, which is separate from planning. Our technical drawings carry the approved design through to construction details your builder and building control can work from.

    Do you work with structural engineers?

    Yes. We bring in a structural engineer for beams, foundations and structural alterations, and coordinate their design into the drawings.

    My application was refused. Can you help?

    Yes. We'll review the decision notice and officer's report, then advise whether a revised scheme or an appeal is the better route.

    Which areas do you cover?

    We're based in Whitstable and work across Kent and London, including Whitstable, Herne Bay, Canterbury, Margate, Ramsgate, Broadstairs, Birchington, Faversham, Sittingbourne, Chatham, Rochester and Gillingham. If you're further afield, get in touch anyway.
