How we use your information

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

Forty-One Architecture is a trading name of Forty-One Consult and Design Ltd, registered in England, company number 16366675. We are responsible for the personal information you send us through this website. If you have any questions about it, email enquiries@forty-onecd.co.uk or call 07404 611105.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, email address, phone number, the site postcode, the type of project and anything you tell us about it.
  • Free call booking: your name, email address, mobile number, the time and type of call you asked for, and any note about your project.
  • Email, phone or WhatsApp: whatever you choose to send us.

Why we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, arrange and hold the call you asked for, and prepare a quote if you want one. We do this because you have asked us to and it is a step towards working together. If you become a client, we use your details to carry out the project.

We will not send you marketing or newsletters, and we never sell or share your details for anyone else's marketing.

Who else sees it

Only us. A small number of service providers handle information on our behalf, under contract and only for the purposes above:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and receives the forms. Netlify is based in the United States, and transfers are protected by standard data protection contract terms approved for UK use.
  • Microsoft, which provides our email and calendar.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message us there.

If your project goes ahead, we may share relevant details with the council, building control and consultants such as structural engineers, but only as needed for your project.

How long we keep it

If you don't go on to become a client, we delete your enquiry details within 12 months. If you do, we keep project records, including contact details, for as long as we need to for legal, insurance and professional reasons, normally six years after the project ends and longer where our professional obligations require it.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. To show the page, your browser fetches fonts from Google Fonts and code from the jsDelivr network, which see your IP address in order to deliver those files.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email enquiries@forty-onecd.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.
